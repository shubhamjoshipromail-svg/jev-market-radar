Breaking news × prediction markets

Radar reads breaking news and flags the markets it moves.

Every headline is checked against the written resolution rules of about 38,000 live Polymarket markets. When the news changes the odds, or settles a market whose price hasn’t caught up, Radar raises an alert. Then it re-checks the price and publishes how each call played out.

How it works

A model judges each pair. Plain code decides what gets flagged.

  1. 01

    Read the news

    13 RSS feeds and 16 Bluesky news accounts, polled continuously. The same story from several outlets is merged, so it’s judged once. You can also paste any headline on the board.

    13 RSS · 16 Bluesky · paste your own
  2. 02

    Narrow to 40

    A keyword prefilter scores the headline against every live market’s question and rules, and keeps about 40 candidates. No model is involved yet.

    38,000 live markets → 40 candidates
  3. 03

    Ask Jev four questions

    For each pair, Jev, TypeSafe AI’s System One model, returns typed answers with probabilities: relevant? same time period? effect on YES? how settled? It never generates text. About 150 ms and $0.00004 per judgment.

    e.g. relevant 0.92 · YES less likely 0.95 · settled fact
  4. 04

    Decide, then keep score

    Code applies fixed thresholds on relevance, confidence, strength, timing, 24-hour volume and current price. What passes becomes a mover or a stale price alert, and its price is re-checked afterwards.

    price checks at +1 · +5 · +10 · +60 min
